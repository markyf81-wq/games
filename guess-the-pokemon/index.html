    A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

    Know your Kanto?

    Identify ten mystery Pokémon from the original 151. Each round begins with one hard clue. Open more clues as you need them, up to five.

    1. Type a name and press Guess or Enter.
    2. A correct answer earns 5, 4, 3, 2 or 1 points, depending on the clue.
    3. A wrong guess opens the next clue. An unrecognised name costs nothing.
    4. After a wrong fifth guess, or if you choose Reveal Pokémon, the answer appears for zero points.

    You can also open the next clue without guessing. Types and evolution clues use the original Kanto games. There is no timer, and each expedition has ten different Pokémon.

    Your best completed expedition and the Pokémon you correctly identify are saved in this browser. An unfinished expedition starts fresh when you reload.
