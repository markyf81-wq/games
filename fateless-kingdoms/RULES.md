## **Quick Start Rules**

### **Goal**

Play up to **3 rounds**.

The first player to win **2 rounds** wins the game.

### **Set Up**

1. Use one shared deck containing one copy of every Unit, Spell and Omen card. Shuffle the deck.  
2. Deal **15 cards** to each player.  
3. Put the rest of the deck face down.  
4. Keep your hand hidden from the other player.

The browser version also offers an optional starting-hand setting, **off by default**: each player receives **exactly 3 random Spell/Omen cards combined**, chosen from whichever of those types are included. Fill all other starting-hand slots with random Unit cards. Extra included Spells and Omens remain in the shared deck and can still be drawn later. Each card is unique; deal without replacement. This option allows 3–27 starting cards per player and is unavailable when both Spells and Omens are excluded. Changes apply only to the next game; normal setup above remains the default.

You only get these 15 cards for the whole game.  
You do not draw new cards between rounds unless a card tells you to draw.

### **The Field**

Each player has a field with **3 rows**:

* **Combat Row:** closest to the enemy  
* **Ranged Row:** in the middle  
* **Siege Row:** closest to you

Unit cards must be placed in the matching row.

The space between both players’ fields is called the **battlefield**.

Some Omen cards are placed on rows. Some are placed in the middle of the battlefield.

### **Card Types**

There are **3 card types**:

**Unit Cards**  
Unit cards have power. Your Unit cards add together to make your score.

Some Unit cards have abilities. Follow the ability when the card is played. Printed card text always takes priority over conflicting wording in this document. Additional rulings apply only where the printed card leaves a detail unspecified.

**Spell Cards**  
Spell cards do something right away.

After using a Spell card, put it on the bottom of the deck.

**Omen Cards**  
Omen cards stay in play.

Some Omens are placed on rows and affect that row.  
Other Omens are placed in the middle of the battlefield and affect both players.

### **Taking Turns**

Choose who goes first: youngest player or random.

Players take turns. On your turn, choose one:

* Play 1 card  
* Pass

You may pass whether you are winning or losing. If your hand is empty after resolving all effects of a play, you automatically pass for the round. This also applies when a new round starts with no cards in hand. If you still hold any cards, do not automatically pass, even when those cards cannot currently be played.

If you pass, you cannot play again that round.  
The other player keeps taking turns until they also pass.

Players take turns going first each round.

### **Winning A Round**

A round ends when both players have passed.

Each player adds up the power of their Unit cards on their field.

The player with the highest total wins the round.

If both players have the same total, the round is a draw and both players get a round win.

### **End Of Round**

At the end of a round, all cards still in play go into the destroyed pile of the player whose field they are on.

Destroyed cards are out of the game unless a card ability brings them back.

## **Extra Details**

### **In Play**

A card is **in play** if it is currently on either player’s field or in the middle of the battlefield.

### **Card Ownership**

If a card is played on your field, it becomes your card while it is there.

If it has power, it counts toward your score.

If it is destroyed or the round ends, it goes into your destroyed pile, even if the other player played it there.

Omen cards in the middle of the battlefield go to the destroyed pile of the player who played them.

### **Drawing Cards**

You do not draw cards between rounds.

Some cards may let you draw from the deck.

Infiltration units may be played when the shared deck is empty; draw nothing. If only one card remains, draw that card instead of two.

### **Omen Effects**

Omen cards stay in play and can change the power of cards or affect rows.

Check Omen effects again after each turn.

When working out card power, apply Omen effects last. First apply weather (Blizzard, Thunderstorm or Flood), setting affected units to 1 power, then add War Chant's \+2 bonus. A unit affected by both weather and War Chant has 3 power, regardless of which Omen was played first. Units protected by Ironward ignore both effects.

### **Not Enough Targets**

If a card says to affect a certain number of cards, but there are not enough cards available, affect as many as possible.

### **No Target**

Distinguish a legal unit placement from the extra effect of a when-played ability. A unit may enter its matching row even when that extra effect has no target. Queen Krath may be played without a Death Gaze target, Restoration units may be played with no matching unit in your destroyed pile, and Infiltration units may be played with an empty shared deck. The unit enters normally and the unavailable effect does nothing. If eligible ability targets exist, choosing a target is still required.

Mimic is an exception: its Convert swap is the required play, so it needs an enemy Combat unit and both Combat rows must allow the swap. It cannot simply enter your field without swapping. Targeted Spells also need a valid play: Decoy needs an eligible friendly unit. Sacrifice needs both a friendly unit outside Sanctuary to destroy and at least one unit already in your destroyed pile to recover. Sacrifice ignores Shielded, including on Stone Wall itself. Pridefall, Weakest Link and Catastrophe need at least one card they can destroy; Purge needs an Omen in play. A destruction Spell can still be played when only one side has eligible targets.

These rules apply equally to both players. Dread still prevents unit entry into a blocked row, even if the unit’s extra ability has no target. Ordinary units and passive abilities can be played in a legal row even if their bonus or protection is not currently useful. Omens can be placed in legal rows or the shared weather area before units arrive, because their effects remain in play. Patience is always a valid play on your turn: spending the turn without passing is its effect.

If you still have cards in hand but none can be played, choose whether to pass; do not automatically pass.

### **Winning The Game**

The first player to win **2 rounds** wins the game.

If both players reach 2 round wins at the same time because of a draw, the game ends in a draw.

## Spell Cards:

* **Decoy:** Return any one of your units in play on your field back to your hand.  
* **Pridefall:** On each side, ignore units protected from this Spell and destroy the highest-power remaining unit. Also destroy all unprotected units tied for that power.  
* **Weakest Link:** Destroy the 3 lowest-power unprotected units in play on each side. If units tie at the cutoff, the player who played Weakest Link chooses which tied units to destroy. Destroy no more than 3 units per side, or as many eligible units as possible if fewer are available.  
* **Patience:** Do nothing. Your turn ends.  
* **Sacrifice:** Destroy any 1 unit card on your field. Bring any 2 unit cards from your destroyed pile into your hand. Only units already in your destroyed pile before this play are eligible to return; the unit sacrificed in this play cannot be returned by the same Sacrifice. You need a friendly unit outside Sanctuary to destroy and at least one previously destroyed unit to recover. Sacrifice ignores Shielded: you may choose a protected Siege unit or Stone Wall itself. If only one recovery target is available, return that one. Units return to your hand, not the field, so their when-played abilities do not trigger. Sanctuary still blocks this Spell, so units in a Sanctuary row cannot be chosen for destruction.  
* **Catastrophe:** Destroy every card in play on both sides.  
* **Purge:** Destroy all OMEN cards in play on both sides.

## Omen Cards:

* **War Chant:** Place on any friendly row. Add 2 power to each unit in that row while this card is in play.  
* **Dread:** Place on any enemy row. No new cards can be added to that row while this card is in play. This also blocks units entering through Convert. Sacrifice returns units to hand, so it does not place units into a row.  
* **Blizzard:** Place in the middle of the battlefield. All Combat units on both sides have their power reduced to 1\.  
* **Thunderstorm:** Place in the middle of the battlefield. All Ranged units on both sides have their power reduced to 1\.  
* **Flood:** Place in the middle of the battlefield. All Siege units on both sides have their power reduced to 1\.  
* **Ironward:** Place on any friendly row. Units in that row are unaffected by all Omen cards, including friendly War Chant bonuses and harmful weather.  
* **Sanctuary:** Place on any friendly row. Units in that row are unaffected by Spell cards.

## Unit Abilities:

* **Infiltration**: Play this unit on your enemy’s field. It adds to their power. Then draw 2 cards into your hand.  
* **Death Gaze**: When played, destroy 1 highest-power enemy RANGED unit. If several enemy RANGED units tie for highest power, the player who played this unit chooses 1 of them to destroy.  
* **Blood Rage:** Gains \+1 power for each enemy COMBAT unit in play. Recalculate after each turn.  
* **Fortified:** Gains \+1 power for each other unit card in your SIEGE row. Do not count this unit or Omen cards. Recalculate after each turn.  
* **Restoration:** When played, choose 1 unit of this unit’s row type from your destroyed pile and return it to your hand.  
* **Accuracy:** Gains \+1 power for each RANGED unit on your field, including this unit itself. Target has 1 power when alone before Omen effects. Recalculate after each turn.  
* **Convert:** Swap the lowest-power enemy COMBAT unit with this card, placing that unit onto your field. If several enemy COMBAT units tie for lowest power, the player who played this unit chooses 1 of them. Dread prevents units from entering a blocked row through this swap.  
* **Shielded:** SIEGE units on your field cannot be destroyed while Stone Wall is in play. This includes Stone Wall itself, as stated by the printed card wording. This protects those units from destruction by either player’s Spells, including Catastrophe, and unit abilities. Your own Sacrifice is the sole exception: it may destroy a Shielded unit, including Stone Wall itself, unless Sanctuary also protects that unit. Weakest Link still respects Shielded even when resolving a tie requires a choice. Round-end cleanup still removes all cards from the field.
