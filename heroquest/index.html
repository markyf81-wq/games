    How to explore

    1. Choose Roll movement once on your hero’s turn. Dice roll in the sidebar; no result confirmation is needed. Red dice use white pips; attack and defence use the skull/shield dice. Heroes press Roll and Zargon rolls automatically.
    2. Click a square and choose Move here in its panel. Hover outlines the tile or model: light blue means it is within movement or action reach, and yellow means it is not; selecting keeps that same colour. The route is blue as far as you can move this turn, then yellow. Choose Move partway here to follow its blue section. Movement follows the grid one square at a time.
    3. Use the wall button above the board to cycle Full walls, Automatic cutaway and Low walls. Cutaway clears walls blocking the active hero’s room; in corridors it protects the hero. Dynamic lighting switches between torch-lit darkness and full tabletop lighting.
    4. When new ground appears, movement pauses so you can review it. Your unspent movement remains.
    5. Click a closed door and choose Open door. Your hero walks into reach first if the route fits your remaining movement. Opening is free and doors stay open.
    6. Heroes may pass through heroes; monsters may pass through monsters. Opposing figures and furniture block ordinary movement. Finish on an empty square. With Pass Through Rock active, select unseen playable squares normally, pass through objects, and reach a free square before your last move or End turn. Unused areas outside the playfield cannot be selected. The stairs have four squares for reach. Steps inside cost no movement but require movement remaining; entering or leaving costs one. This discount is our digital adaptation.
    7. Click a monster and choose Attack, then press Roll in the sidebar. Your defence and spell resistance rolls also wait for Roll. Results return to play automatically. Attack once with your starting weapon against a monster directly beside you, across an open edge. Skulls hit; heroes defend on white shields and monsters on black shields.
    8. Move then act, or act then move. Never move, act, then resume moving. Attacking or casting first still allows movement afterwards; acting after movement ends the remaining movement. Opening doors is free.
    9. Every revealed piece opens a panel with its name and description. Unavailable actions stay visible and explain why they are disabled. Shift + arrows selects a known square, [ / ] cycles revealed pieces, and Enter opens the panel; Escape closes it.
    10. After defeating Verag, return to the stairway and choose Leave dungeon to complete that hero’s quest. Leaving earlier is a retreat. Other heroes can continue; the session ends when everyone has left or fallen.
    11. End your turn and hand the device to the next hero still in the dungeon. After the party, press Continue on Zargon’s turn screen to let the revealed monsters act. Their moves and rolls then play automatically; Pause Zargon pauses between stages. Hero defence rolls still wait for your click.

    Elf and Wizard: choose Spells to cast instead of attacking. Browse the original spell cards using the arrows or swipe. The spell drawer stays beside the board. Choose a target on the glowing board to cast; cast cards leave your hand. Gameplay sandbox opens a separate practice save; Test room remains the model showroom. Inventory shows each hero’s equipment, gold and potions. Drink uses no action or movement; healing includes one red die. Search for treasure uses one action in a monster-free room, once per hero per room. It reveals an original card or a quest find directly. Hazards end your turn; wandering monsters attack immediately if adjacent. Heroic Brew allows a second attack; Finish attacks declines it. Use Search in the sidebar to choose treasure, traps or secret doors. Trap and secret-door searches require no visible monster. Jump over costs two moves and a roll. Disarm a found, unsprung trap on a later turn using the Dwarf or a Tool Kit. Successful disarming allows remaining movement. Secret doors slide open for free from an adjacent square. The sandbox includes exploration practice scenarios. Thrown weapons remain unfinished.
