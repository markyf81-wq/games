    How to play

    HeroQuest is a cooperative dungeon adventure. Guide your hero through hidden rooms, fight monsters and uncover treasure. The heroes work towards a shared quest objective, while Zargon commands the monsters.

    The adventure

    Choose Quest info beside the quest title to read your mission. Explore together, look for useful items and watch your heroes’ hearts: they show how much health remains. Healing potions can help an injured hero survive.

    At zero hearts, use your own healing if it is offered. Otherwise that hero is out for the rest of the quest. A companion in the same room or corridor can recover their possessions; otherwise the possessions are lost. With optional Fair loot recovery, fallen heroes instead leave sealed bags and revive after a successful quest. Their possessions return only if the bag’s carrier completes the quest.

    Complete the objective and return to the stairs. Click them and choose Leave dungeon to bring your hero to safety. You can also retreat early if the danger becomes too great.

    Rounds and turns

    Heroes take turns in the order shown in The party. On your turn, you can move and take an action, such as attacking, casting a spell or searching. You can act before or after moving.

    After the heroes finish, Zargon takes a turn with the monsters. The game controls Zargon. Once that turn ends, a new round begins with the heroes.

    Your turn

    1. Move. Choose Roll movement, then click a tile and choose Move here.
    2. Act. Click a door, monster or other piece to see its actions. Choose Open door, Attack or another available action. Press Roll when prompted.
    3. Finish. Choose End turn when you are ready. On Zargon’s turn screen, choose Continue.

    More actions

    • Search: choose Search, then treasure, traps or secret doors. Read any treasure card and choose Continue.
    • Spells: open Spells, browse the cards, choose Choose target and click a highlighted target.
    • Inventory: open Inventory to read item cards, equip gear, drink potions or give an item to an adjacent hero. Neither hero can be beside a monster when trading.
    • Traps: click a discovered trap for Jump over or Disarm trap.

    Between quests

    After the party leaves the dungeon, choose Visit Armoury on the results screen. Select a hero to buy equipment with their gold, or choose Sell / equip to sell one item at a time or change their gear.

    Look around

    Drag to rotate, Shift-drag to pan, and scroll or pinch to zoom. Click your hero’s portrait to centre the view. The board button toggles top view; click it again to return to your previous view. POV follows the acting unit from directly behind. The eye button highlights whole tiles with clear sight from the active hero. Starting an action turns it off; click the eye again when you want to check sight. In the Adventure menu, Show full board keeps the printed board visible while unexplored walls and pieces remain hidden.
