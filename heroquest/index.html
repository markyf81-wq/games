    AT THE TABLE

    Adventure menu

    How to explore

    1. Choose Roll movement, then press Roll in the dice modal once on your hero’s turn. Red dice use white pips; attack and defence use the skull/shield dice. Heroes press Roll and Zargon rolls automatically.
    2. Click a square and choose Move here in its panel. Hover outlines the tile or model: light blue means it is within movement or action reach, and yellow means it is not; selecting keeps that same colour. Movement follows the grid one square at a time.
    3. When new ground appears, movement pauses so you can review it. Your unspent movement remains.
    4. Click a closed door and choose Open door when adjacent. Opening is free and doors stay open.
    5. Pass through friendly heroes, but finish on an empty square. Monsters and furniture block movement. The whole 2×2 stairway is one shared space: moving inside it is free, and stepping off costs one movement. This shared-space rule is our digital adaptation.
    6. Click a monster and choose Attack, then roll in the modal. Attack once with your starting weapon against a monster directly beside you, across an open edge. Skulls hit; heroes defend on white shields and monsters on black shields.
    7. You may attack before moving, or move and then attack. Attacking after any movement ends your remaining movement.
    8. Every revealed piece opens a panel with its name and description. Unavailable actions stay visible and explain why they are disabled. Shift + arrows selects a known square, [ / ] cycles revealed pieces, and Enter opens the panel; Escape closes it.
    9. End your turn and hand the device to the next living hero. After the party, Zargon controls the revealed monsters. Continue through the result screens to review their moves and attacks.

    Spells, healing, searches and thrown weapons are not available yet. This makes combat harder for the Elf and Wizard. Progress saves automatically in this browser and resumes on refresh. Formal quest completion is not available.
