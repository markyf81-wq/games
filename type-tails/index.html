WELCOME TO THE CLUB

A little how-to.

  1. Choose your level. Start with Beginner. Complete a full round at the displayed WPM target on the previous difficulty to unlock the next one. Points earn shop coins.
  2. Practice 20 words. Each round picks 20 different words from a larger practice pool and shuffles their order. Words use the keys introduced at your difficulty: home row first, then upper and lower reaches, then common word patterns. Every replay draws a fresh selection. Match the big word exactly. Correct words submit automatically. Backspace fixes mistakes.
  3. Beat the bar. The timer resets for every word. Faster words earn more points. If time runs out, move on and keep trying.
  4. Make it yours. Points also become spendable coins. Shop between rounds for Pip's outfits and room decorations. Spending never lowers your lifetime points.

Each correct word earns 40–100 base points (depending on difficulty), up to 80 speed points, and a streak bonus up to 25. Use Esc to pause.

Music starts when you first interact. The ♫ and ♪ buttons switch music and sound effects on or off. Progress is saved in this browser.
