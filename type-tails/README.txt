TYPE & TAILS

Open index.html in Google Chrome to play. No install, build, server, or internet connection is required. Keep index.html, styles.css, and game.js together.

Choose a difficulty before a round. Each round draws 20 different words from an expanded practice pool and shuffles their order. Restarting or replaying a difficulty draws a fresh selection. Words stay within the keys introduced at that difficulty. Correct words submit automatically. Backspace fixes mistakes. The countdown at the bottom resets for each word. Esc pauses, and moving to another browser tab pauses automatically.

Correct words earn a base reward, up to 80 speed points, and up to 25 streak points. Each point also earns one coin. Points become shop coins. Complete a full 20-word round at the required speed on the previous difficulty to unlock the next one permanently. Spending coins never changes your speed records or earned levels. Early finishes do not record a qualifying speed.

WPM uses correct characters divided by five, over active word time; time spent on missed words counts, pauses and transitions do not. Existing levels unlocked before this update remain available.
The shop opens between rounds. Purchases equip automatically. Owned items can be equipped or put away for free. One hat, rug, or wall color is equipped at a time; other decorations can be combined. Purchased rugs replace the plain rug underneath Pip: rose has cream diamonds and meadow has a leafy pattern. The shop preview matches the room design; existing purchases use these designs automatically.

PRACTICE PROGRESSION
The eight practice pools contain 53, 118, 117, 123, 125, 108, 165, and 114 eligible words respectively.
Beginner: home-row foundations, with index fingers resting on F and J.
Explorer: add E, R, U, and I.
Adventurer: add T, O, and P.
Wordsmith: add Q, W, and Y.
Trailblazer: add C, V, B, N, and M.
Expert: complete the keyboard with Z and X.
Master: common TH, SH, CH, and ST letter patterns.
Legend: everyday word families and ER, ING, ED, and FUL endings.

Music and effects are generated by Web Audio. The background arrangement has four sections and a varied second pass. The room stays calm, with a roughly 3 minute 35 second cycle. During a level the music becomes a little faster and brighter, with a light beat and bass line; its cycle lasts about 2 minutes 54 seconds. Pausing or finishing returns to the calm room music. Music begins after your first click or key press. The music (♫) and sound effects (♪) controls are independent. Your browser saves points, coins, purchases, decorations, difficulty selection, per-level completion counts, per-level speed records, earned levels, and audio preferences automatically.
