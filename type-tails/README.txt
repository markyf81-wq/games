TYPE & TAILS

Open index.html in Google Chrome to play. No install, build, server, or internet connection is required. Keep index.html, styles.css, and game.js together.

Choose a difficulty before a round. Each round has 20 randomly selected words. Correct words submit automatically. Backspace fixes mistakes. The countdown at the bottom resets for each word. Esc pauses, and moving to another browser tab pauses automatically.

Correct words earn a base reward, up to 80 speed points, and up to 25 streak points. Each point also earns one coin. Lifetime points unlock levels permanently; spending coins does not reduce them.

The shop opens between rounds. Purchases equip automatically. Owned items can be equipped or put away for free. One hat, rug, or wall color is equipped at a time; other decorations can be combined.

Music and effects are generated by Web Audio. Music begins after your first click or key press. The music (♫) and sound effects (♪) controls are independent. Your browser saves points, coins, purchases, decorations, difficulty selection, and audio preferences automatically.
